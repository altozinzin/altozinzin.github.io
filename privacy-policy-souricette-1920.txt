 PRIVACY POLICY - SOURICETTE 1920                       (English version)
 Version française plus bas dans ce document.
==============================================================================

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Souricette 1920 ("the Game", "the app") is an Android game published by
[PUBLISHER NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game
processes, why, who receives it, and what choices you have.

Contact (privacy questions, data requests): [CONTACT E-MAIL]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No account, no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number,
  contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.
- The Game stores your progress on your device (nickname, scores, lives).
- The Game sends to our server: a random installation ID, your nickname and
  your scores (see section 3).
- Unless you buy the unlock, the Game displays ads served by Google AdMob,
  which may use your advertising ID (see section 4).
- Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kept locally (app storage), never leaving your device unless stated in
section 3:
- Nickname you type in the menu (max. 14 characters).
- Best scores (today, this month, all time) and the name tied to your record.
- Number of lives, time of the next life, time the last ad ended.
- Purchase status ("game unlocked"), sound on/off setting.
- A random installation ID (a UUID generated at first launch). It is not
  linked to your identity, your device ID, your advertising ID or your
  Google account.
- Technical flags (e.g. whether the notification permission was requested).
You can erase all of this by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
If Android's backup is enabled on your device, this data may be saved to
your Google account by the system (Android Auto Backup) and restored when
you set up a new device. You control this in your device settings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DATA SENT TO OUR SERVER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Server: serveurzinzin.hopto.org (operated by us).

a) When a game starts: your random installation ID. Purpose: count how many
   games are played (statistics, game improvement).
b) When a game ends: installation ID, nickname, score and highest floor
   reached. The server keeps, for each installation and each day, your best
   score of that day (with nickname and floor).
   Purpose: display the best scores of all players (today, this month, all
   time).
c) When the Game Over screen opens: the Game requests the best scores of all
   players (score and nickname) from the server.
d) As with any internet request, our server receives your IP address and
   standard technical information (date/time, request). Server logs are
   used for security and troubleshooting only.

Important:
- The NICKNAME AND SCORE of the holders of the best scores (today, this month,
  all time) are shown publicly to every player. Do not use your real name or any personal information as nickname.
- Requests to our server are currently sent over HTTP (not encrypted).
  The data involved is limited (random ID, nickname, score) but could be
  observed on an untrusted network.
- We do not use this data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.
  We do not sell it.
- Retention: statistics are kept as long as the Game is operated; records
  are kept as long as they are displayed or needed for statistics. You may
  ask for deletion at any time (section 9).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The free version shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile
Ads SDK): an interstitial ad after you lose a life (not more often than every
2 minutes) and an optional rewarded ad to get an extra life.
No ads are shown once you have purchased the unlock, and the ads SDK is no
longer started at launch.

AdMob and Google may collect and use, as independent controller/processor
as described in their policies: your advertising ID, IP address, device and
app information (model, OS version, language), ad interaction and diagnostic
data, to deliver, measure and secure ads and prevent fraud.
- Consent: where required (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland), a consent form
  (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown at launch. You can change your
  choice later with the "Privacy settings" button in the main menu when it is
  displayed.
- Ad personalization: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of
  personalized ads in Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads.
- More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and
  https://policies.google.com/privacy

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. IN-APP PURCHASE (GOOGLE PLAY BILLING)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The one-time purchase "unlock_souricette" (unlimited lives, no ads) is
processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.
The Game only receives from Google Play the information that the purchase was
completed (product ID and purchase token) to unlock the game and to restore
it ("Restore purchases"). See https://policies.google.com/privacy

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. NOTIFICATIONS, VIBRATION AND OTHER DEVICE FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Notifications (Android 13+ asks your permission): local reminders to beat
  your record, scheduled 1 day, 1 week and 2 weeks after you open the Game
  (timers restart at each launch). They are generated on your device; no
  data is sent to create them. They are shown only when your screen is on,
  so they do not wake you up at night. You can disable them in Android
  settings > Apps > Souricette 1920 > Notifications.
- Vibration: haptic feedback when you catch cheese, during the turbo bonus
  and when you lose. No data is collected. You can disable vibration in your
  device settings.
- Network access: required for ads, purchases, statistics and the world
  record. The Game can be played without a connection, except for these
  features.
- Screen: the screen stays on while the Game is in the foreground.
- The Game does not access your location, contacts, files, photos,
  microphone, camera or SMS.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. LEGAL BASES (EEA / UK / SWITZERLAND)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Statistics, best scores, security of the server: our legitimate interest
  in operating and improving the Game (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Personalized ads and use of advertising ID: your consent (art. 6(1)(a)),
  collected through the Google consent form, which you can withdraw at any
  time.
- Purchase processing: performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b)), carried out
  by Google Play.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. SHARING AND INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with:
- Google (AdMob, Google Play Billing, UMP consent): as described above;
  Google may process data in countries outside your own, under the
  safeguards described in its policies.
- Our hosting provider, which hosts the server.
- Authorities, if required by law.
Nickname and score of the holders of the best scores are visible to all players.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Depending on your country (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss law, CCPA/CPRA, etc.), you
may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data,
to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data
protection authority.
To exercise them, e-mail [CONTACT E-MAIL] and include your nickname and your
score, so we can find your data. The installation ID is random, is not shown
in the app and is not linked to your identity: we can therefore only
identify server-side data through the nickname and score you give us, and we
cannot identify data we cannot link to you.
We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA.
Local data can be erased by clearing the app data or uninstalling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. CHILDREN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable
under local law), and it is not designed for families. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has
provided a nickname or other data, contact us and we will delete it.
Ads are requested without any child-directed treatment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We limit the data collected and restrict server access. No transmission or
storage is 100 % secure; in particular, requests to our server are not yet
encrypted (see section 3).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We may update this policy, for example when features change. The date at the
top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the
store listing or in the app.

Serveur : serveurzinzin.hopto.org (exploité par nous).

   journaux du serveur servent uniquement à la sécurité et au dépannage.

- Le PSEUDO ET LE SCORE des détenteurs des meilleurs scores (du jour, du mois,
  de tous les temps) sont affichés publiquement à tous les joueurs. N'utilisez pas votre vrai nom ni

- Les requêtes vers notre serveur sont actuellement envoyées en HTTP (non
  chiffré). Les données concernées sont limitées (identifiant aléatoire,
  pseudo, score) mais pourraient être observées sur un réseau non fiable.
- Nous n'utilisons pas ces données pour la publicité, le profilage ou des
  décisions automatisées. Nous ne les vendons pas.
- Conservation : les statistiques sont conservées tant que le Jeu est
