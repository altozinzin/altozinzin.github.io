Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Altozinzin ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our Android mobile game (the "Game"). By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Gameplay and Progress Data

The Game collects limited gameplay and progress information, including the number of games played.

This information is used to maintain and improve the Game and to obtain anonymous statistics about game usage.

The gameplay statistics sent to our server are not associated with a user account, name, email address, or other information that we use to directly identify a player.

The Game does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, telephone number, or other directly identifying information in order to play.

Advertising-Related Data

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process certain information from the device and from interactions with the Game in connection with advertising services. Depending on the device, operating system, advertising settings, and applicable consent or privacy settings, this may include information such as:

  • IP address;
  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers;
  • Information about interactions with advertisements and the Game;
  • Diagnostic and performance information; and
  • Other information required to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

This information may be collected directly by Google or its advertising partners through the advertising SDK.

We do not use this information to create a personal account or directly identify individual players.

2. Advertising

The Game displays:

  • Interstitial advertisements; and
  • Rewarded advertisements that may provide additional lives or gameplay bonuses.

Advertising is provided through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google may use information processed through its advertising services to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, maintain security, and comply with applicable requirements.

Advertising practices may vary depending on the user's location, age-related treatment, device settings, consent choices, and applicable laws.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please consult Google's privacy documentation.

3. Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically designed exclusively for children, but it may be accessible to users of all ages, including children.

The Game does not knowingly request children's names, email addresses, telephone numbers, precise location, or user accounts.

Because the Game may be used by children, advertising and data processing are subject to the applicable requirements of Google Play's Families policies and applicable privacy laws.

We do not knowingly use children's personal information to create personalized advertising profiles.

Advertising provided to children or users whose age is unknown is intended to comply with applicable restrictions concerning interest-based advertising and children's advertising.

Parents or legal guardians who have questions or concerns about the privacy practices of the Game may contact us using the information provided below.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party service:

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — used to provide interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Google may collect and process information through its SDK in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy information for additional details regarding data processed by Google Mobile Ads.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

The anonymous gameplay statistics collected by our server are used by us for the operation and improvement of the Game.

Advertising-related information may be transmitted directly from the Game to Google and its advertising partners through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Such processing is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the gameplay information transmitted to and stored on our server.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

The anonymous gameplay statistics collected by our server are retained indefinitely.

Because these statistics are not associated with a player account or directly identifying information, we do not maintain individual user profiles based on this information.

Data processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's applicable data retention policies.

8. Data Deletion

The Game does not provide user accounts, and the gameplay statistics collected by us are not associated with identifiable user profiles.

As a result, we generally cannot identify or delete a particular user's anonymous gameplay statistics.

Third-party advertising data is handled according to the policies and controls provided by Google.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, our data practices, third-party services, or applicable legal and platform requirements.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.
